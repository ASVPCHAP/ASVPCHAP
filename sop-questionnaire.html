SOP Questionnaire

Help us document how your auxiliary actually runs. Pick your auxiliary below — if it's already been through an assessment, we'll show what we know so far so you're not repeating yourself. Answer whatever you can; you can always come back and add more later.

Your info
1. Purpose & Scope

In one or two sentences, what does your auxiliary do and why does it matter? What's outside your scope (handled by another auxiliary instead)?

2. Roles & Responsibilities

Who's on your team right now, and what does each person specifically handle?

3. Pre-Service / Setup

What has to happen before doors open or before service starts? Walk through it in order — first, second, third. What time does your team need to arrive?

4. During-Service Duties

What does your team actually do while service is happening? What are you watching for or responding to?

5. Post-Service / Breakdown

What has to happen after service ends before you can leave — cleanup, equipment storage, lock-up?

6. Supplies & Equipment

What do you need on hand to do this job? Where is it stored? What do you do when you're running low?

7. Emergency & Safety Procedures *

If something goes wrong during service — medical issue, security concern, fire, severe weather — what do you do? Who do you notify first? If there's no procedure yet, say so — that's useful to know too.

8. Scheduling & Coverage

How does your team know when they're serving? How do you communicate updates or handle someone needing to swap or miss a date?

9. Training & Onboarding

If someone brand new joined your team tomorrow, what would they need to know or do before serving on their own?

10. Special Circumstances

Are there specific situations unique to your auxiliary that come up regularly (communion Sundays, guest speakers, holidays, off-site events)?
