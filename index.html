    The pharmacist's edge

    Most coaches know training. Kevin knows how the body works.

    A pharmacist's training is years of physiology, biochemistry and how substances interact with the human body. That background shows up in every plan.

    No hype stacks. Kevin recommends what the research supports, in the order it matters, and only from brands he has personally used and trusts.

    Health-aware programming

    Blood pressure, blood sugar, sleep, stress and the medications you already take all shape how your body responds. Your plan accounts for that.

    Nutrient timing done right

    How it works

    Simple system. Relentless consistency.

    There is no secret. There is a plan, a weekly check-in, and a coach who adjusts the plan based on what your body actually does.

    Apply & consult

    Tell Kevin your goal, history and schedule. You get a free consult and an honest answer about what it will take.

    Get your plan

    Custom macros (training, rest and high-carb days), food lists, supplement guidance and, on Transformation and Prep, your full training program.

    Execute & track

    Weigh your food, log every bite, hit your steps. Weigh-ins and waist measurements first thing in the morning.

    Saturday check-in

    Send the feedback form and side-by-side progress photos. Kevin reviews and sends adjustments for the week ahead.

    Accountability, weekly

    Your Saturday check-in

    Every week you fill out the True Power feedback sheet. Nothing hides. The numbers tell the story and the photos confirm it.

    • Body weight and waist, measured first thing in the morning
    • Cardio type and sessions completed
    • Compliance score, honest, 1 to 10
    • Water intake and average sleep per night
    • Front, back and side photos, this week next to last week
    • Weeks-out tracking for competitors
