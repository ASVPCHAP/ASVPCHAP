For Auxiliary Leaders

Read this before you fill out the questionnaire

This handbook is a work in progress. What's documented so far was pulled together from an initial team assessment and a recorded walkthrough of several auxiliaries — it's a starting point, not a finished record, and it may be incomplete, outdated, or not quite how your auxiliary actually runs day to day. We're sending this out to every auxiliary leader to help us close those gaps.

  1. Look up your auxiliary first. Go to Auxiliaries, find your team, and read through what's already there — contacts, SOP, supplies, and notes.
  2. Correct or add detail directly on that page. If your auxiliary already has an SOP but something's missing, wrong, or could use more detail, use the buttons on that page (+ Add SOP Notes, + Add a Contact, + Request Supplies, + Add a Note) rather than starting over.
  3. If your auxiliary has no SOP yet, fill out the questionnaire. Use the SOP Questionnaire to help us build yours from scratch — it walks through the same sections (setup, duties, safety, supplies, scheduling, and more) so nothing important gets missed.

Submitting a form doesn't publish instantly — every submission is reviewed and folded in so the finished page stays clear and doesn't just pile up as scattered notes.
